IkiminaPass

Architected and led the full backend of IkiminaPass, a fintech platform digitising Rwanda's community savings groups (Ibimina). Built on NestJS, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM, the system handles automated contribution tracking, loan management, and group treasury operations. Integrated IntouchPay and RSwitch/MoMo for mobile money processing, built a USSD interface for feature-phone access, and wired Africa's Talking SMS alongside FCM for dual-channel real-time alerts — making the platform accessible to members across all device types.
